FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the shipping charges?

We offer free shipping on all orders above ₹4,999 within India. For orders below this amount, a nominal shipping fee of ₹99 applies.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 7-14 business days. Express delivery (3-5 days) is available for select pin codes at an additional charge.

Can I return a saree if I don't like it?

Yes, we offer a 7-day return policy on all unused sarees. Please ensure the saree is in its original condition with all tags intact.

Do you offer customization or stitching?

Yes, we offer customization services for blouses and can also adjust the saree length according to your preference. Please contact us for details.
