Our Journey

From a Small Shop to a Global Legacy

What started as a small saree store in the heart of Tamil Nadu has now grown into a beloved brand — cherished by women across the globe. Our commitment to quality, authenticity, and customer delight has remained unwavering through generations.

Every saree we sell is a piece of art — woven by skilled artisans who have inherited this craft from their ancestors. We work directly with weavers to bring you the finest kanjivaram, banarasi, and ikat sarees at the most affordable prices.

Milestones

Our Journey Through Time

1928

The Beginning

Founded as a small saree shop in Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu, with a vision to bring authentic silk to every Indian woman.

1965

Expansion to Chennai

Opened our first flagship store in Chennai, introducing the city to our exquisite Kanjivaram collection.

1998

Going Digital

Embraced technology to make our sarees accessible to women across India through our first online catalog.

2024

Global Presence

Now shipping to over 50 countries, bringing the beauty of Indian silk to the world.
